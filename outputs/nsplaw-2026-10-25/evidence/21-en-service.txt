DISPUTES

NSP lawyers and attorneys-at-law plead cases in Russian state courts and international arbitration, coordinate proceedings in foreign state courts and manage disputes with state authorities. We are skilled in handling parallel litigation in various jurisdictions and develop the strategy based on the actual enforcement.

Main areas of specialization:
Pre-trial dispute resolution
Development of a litigation strategy
Commercial and property disputes in Russian courts
International commercial arbitration
Corporate conflicts
Disputes in the securities market
Bankruptcy and subsidiary liability
Disputes with state authorities, including tax disputes and disputes with the Federal Antimonopoly Service
Disputes under government contracts
Disputes related to the integrated area development
